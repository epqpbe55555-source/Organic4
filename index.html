Golden rules of electrophilic aromatic substitution

  1. Every reaction needs an electrophile strong enough to attack the electron-rich ring.
  2. Ring attack — formation of the arenium ion — is always the slow, rate-determining step.
  3. Proton loss that restores the aromatic ring is always fast; aromaticity is the driving force.
  4. Positive charge in the arenium ion sits ortho and para to the new group — never meta.
  5. A Lewis or Brønsted acid (H₂SO₄, AlCl₃, FeCl₃) is usually needed to generate or polarize the electrophile.
  6. Equilibrium reactions (sulfonation ⇌ desulfonation) can be pushed either way by changing acid strength and water/steam conditions.

Nitration

Electrophile: NO₂⁺
  • H₂SO₄ + HNO₃ ⇌ H₃O⁺ + 2 HSO₄⁻ + NO₂⁺
  • H₂SO₄ = acid, HNO₃ = base in this equilibrium
  • Nitronium salts (NO₂⁺ClO₄⁻, NO₂⁺BF₄⁻) nitrate arenes directly at room temperature (Olah)

Sulfonation

Electrophile: SO₃
  • SO₃ is electron-deficient — a Lewis acid, despite no formal charge
  • Fuming/concentrated H₂SO₄ (excess SO₃) drives the forward reaction
  • Product: benzenesulfonic acid — a strong, highly dissociated acid

Desulfonation

Electrophile: H⁺
  • Exact reverse of sulfonation (microscopic reversibility)
  • Dilute acid + superheated steam, 100–175 °C
  • Removing volatile hydrocarbon by steam distillation pulls equilibrium backward

Friedel–Crafts Alkylation

Electrophile: R⁺
  • RCl + AlCl₃ ⇌ R⁺ + AlCl₄⁻ (Lewis acid–base equilibrium)
  • Alternative path: R⁺ transferred directly from the polar complex, no free carbocation
  • AlCl₃ and HCl regenerated — catalytic cycle

Friedel–Crafts Acylation

Electrophile: RCO⁺
  • Acylium ion formed from AlCl₃ + acyl chloride complex, C–Cl cleavage
  • Resonance-stabilized across C and O
  • Product (aryl ketone) deactivates the ring — stops at monoacylation

Halogenation

Electrophile: Cl⁺ (as complex)
  • Cl₂ + FeCl₃ → polarized complex, no free Cl⁺ ever forms
  • Chlorine transfers to the ring without its bonding electrons
  • Very electron-rich arenes react with X₂ even without a catalyst
